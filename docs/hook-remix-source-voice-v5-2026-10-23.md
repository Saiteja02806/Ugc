## Prompt direction

Remix as the same creator making the same kind of joke or discovery in a relevant new situation. Preserve the source's social setup, emotional turn, sentence shape, cadence, emphasis, punctuation, useful line breaks and punchline position. Adapt the subject only as far as relevance and truthful claims require. Business context enters through the situation or payoff rather than a mandatory product explanation.

Deliberate EVERYTHING, rhetorical LEGAL, an unresolved this and conversational emoji can survive. Read their purpose instead of removing them categorically. Clearly fictional POV/personification is different from asserting real lived experience. Remove unsupported numbers/results/history while keeping the source's emotional beat. There is no fixed 5–10-word target or required AI/brand/approval mention. Character/block limits still match the application output contract.

The closing review asks whether the remix still lands on the same kind of joke, surprise or feeling; whether its source is recognizable beyond the POV prefix; and whether it could be substituted into several unrelated sources unchanged. A feature-list-like answer should be rewritten. These are writing instructions, not a validated semantic quality gate.
