Priority below means workflow relevance, not measured search volume, brand popularity or proven ranking opportunity. No competitor accounts, paid renders or side-by-side exports were tested.

## Official evidence checked

- [Revid about](https://www.revid.ai/about) and [features](https://www.revid.ai/features): documents product/launch videos, editing, scheduled publishing and agent access. Strong audience match. Treat its output and performance claims as vendor statements; do not reuse its criticism of other tools as independent evidence.
- [Postjet](https://postjet.io/): documents slideshow, wall-of-text and hook-plus-demo formats, brand context and review. Its FAQ describes TikTok inbox completion, so a broad publishing headline does not establish direct TikTok publication. This is postjet.io, not the separate postjet.app service. Counts and feature lists vary across sections; avoid a numerical format-count comparison.
- [UGCPoster](https://ugcposter.com/): describes combining supplied reactions and app demos, writing hooks and scheduled distribution. Do not turn its illustrative earnings/time examples into evidence of customer results, or infer it cannot generate anything else solely from the homepage's focus.
- [AITuber reaction-hook help](https://help.aituber.app/en/article/ugc-reaction-hooks-tk0fds/): documents optional demo upload after a reaction, text editing and a muted demo soundtrack. This is a precise behavior worth testing against the same source file.
- [Predis auto-posting help](https://help.predis.ai/knowledgebase/en/article/get-started-with-auto-posting-using-predis): documents multiple media types, a business brief, an editable plan and platform conditions. [Homepage](https://predis.ai/) currently emphasizes ads; don't describe it as merely a scheduler.
- [Creatify app-ad use case](https://creatify.ai/use-cases/app-ads) and [Arcads](https://www.arcads.ai/): support an app-ad comparison later, not an assertion of identical recurring organic-content workflows.
- [ReelFarm](https://reel.farm/): slideshow automation is its core positioning, but it also advertises AI UGC selfies/images/video. The owner's experience was slideshow-focused; that should not become an absolute public claim that ReelFarm has no other formats.

UGC Pilot evidence: current authenticated Explore page visibly lists Talking Head + Demo, Hook video, Wall of text and Slideshows; public pricing/guide pages passed live checks earlier today. Menu visibility does not establish end-to-end export quality. `lib/trending/format-policy.ts` limits recurring Trending content to slideshows. Do not imply all Explore formats arrive in the daily feed. Exact client integration coverage requires separate acceptance.

## First article brief: Revid.ai vs UGC Pilot for SaaS and app videos

Proposed path: `/compare/revid-vs-ugc-pilot` (not created or published).

Reader: founder choosing how to turn a real product task into short social content. Opening: name their inputs—website, screen recording, approved hook words—and the finished file they need. Disclosure: UGC Pilot authors this comparison; documentation-based sections are labelled.

Structure:

1. Choose the task: product demo, recurring slideshows or presenter-led clip.
2. Compare accepted inputs and the work between a first draft and usable export.
3. Show an equivalent task with the same permitted media. Preserve the real product interface; generated screens are not product proof.
4. Compare exact wording, editing, audio, crop and saved-draft recovery.
5. Separate exported files, scheduling and actual publication by platform.
6. Explain plan price, credits, retries and active editing effort separately. Do not equate different credit currencies or claim cheapest usable output without measurements.
7. State who should evaluate each tool, including cases favoring the competitor.
8. Link the practical demo guide as the next step.

Missing evidence: authenticated competitor workflow, equivalent final files, active work/elapsed time, actual spend and current checkout restrictions. A documented feature comparison can be written without these; a hands-on winner cannot. No exclusive MCP, editing, business-context or approval claim is supported.

## Second article brief: Postjet vs UGC Pilot for recurring app content

Compare one message as a slideshow and one opening-plus-demo task separately. Check how business context is revised, media rights recorded, words corrected and destinations chosen. Explain the TikTok inbox handoff explicitly. Test recurring-feed formats separately from one-off creation formats. Do not claim UGC Pilot wins on variety without checking actual available workflows and outputs in both accounts.

## Publication evidence checklist

- Each feature cell has an official source or a dated capture.
- Unverified means unverified, not absent.
- Any hands-on claim includes permitted inputs, final artifacts and recorded effort/spend.
- Prices and account/platform restrictions rechecked on publication day.
- No invented customer outcomes, performance scores or search demand.
- Unique reader question and useful tradeoffs for every page; add public routes only when the article is complete.
