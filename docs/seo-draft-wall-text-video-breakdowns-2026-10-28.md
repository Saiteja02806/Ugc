# Draft: Wall-of-text video examples for software founders

**Unpublished planning draft.** Original evidence required before release: Permissioned complete overlays and clips with reading/motion annotations. Fictional examples are labeled; they are not actual customer outcomes.

## Choose a complete clip and its approved words

A meaningful wall-of-text breakdown shows the full approved overlay, its timing and the moving footage beneath it. Use an asset you own or have permission to show. A still image alone cannot establish that every line stays readable throughout the clip.

## Mark reading and motion

Record the exact copy and actual start and end times. Note when hands, faces, interface elements or bright areas pass behind the text. Ask a reviewer to read the clip at normal speed on a phone and capture their real feedback without inventing a quote.

## Illustrative overlay

A fictional planning example might read “the call is over. the follow-up is tomorrow. open the project note and check the next step.” A calm workspace background could support the situation. This is a writing example, not an existing clip or a customer testimonial.

## Present the evidence accurately

For a real breakdown include the permissioned clip, exact words, relevant annotated frames and the limitations of the review. Compare the export to the approved text and check rights for music and recognizable people. Do not claim a measured performance result that was not observed.
