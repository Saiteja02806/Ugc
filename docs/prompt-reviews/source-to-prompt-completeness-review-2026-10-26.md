## Required comparison for every reference

Use a row for each meaningful source interval: exact source timestamp/index, visible action and hold, gaze/expression, hand/prop contact, camera behavior, current assembled prompt clause, omission/contradiction, proposed correction, confidence and review status. Map the complete original timeline to the requested duration. Keep observed evidence separate from inferred physical causes.

Check before/during/after transitions and every ending frame. Distinguish camera movement from subject movement and digital zoom. A source that is stable should retain that stability; never add phone handling or shake to unrelated sources. Keep real cuts, repeated gestures, offscreen uncertainty and final holds/movements source-specific. Still-image prompts depict the opening only; motion timing belongs to animation prompts. Middle-frame clarity timestamps remain preparation evidence, not output-start substitutions.

Inspect the final assembled provider prompt, not just draft prose. Numeric phase coverage is a structural check, not proof that small but important source details are present. Compare actual compiled ordinary/middle routes, user edits and selected duration before proposing runtime activation.
