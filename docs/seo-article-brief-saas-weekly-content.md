## Reader problem and outcome

A SaaS founder struggles to choose useful organic content and explain ideas
clearly across a week. They need a practical way to turn real customer
questions into distinct posts that teach something. A verified product feature
can provide concrete evidence for the explanation.

After reading, the founder should have a filled message brief, three draft
content assets, a realistic weekly schedule, and a way to evaluate responses
and whether the posts helped readers understand or act. Product interest is
an additional signal where it matches the post's purpose.

Working title: **SaaS Content Marketing: Turn Customer Questions into a Week
of Useful Posts**.

## Editorial approach

The current guide explains sources, content jobs, adapting ideas, and keeping
a library. Its improvement should be a complete example with usable outputs
for organic publishing. Explain how to find customer questions, select a
useful takeaway, choose a format, and evaluate the response. Include common
mistakes and a copyable planning template. The article should remain useful
with the reader's existing tools; UGC Pilot is a relevant worked example.
Use the real Hook video, Wall of text, and Slideshows flows once verified on
production and documented with the user's screenshots. Until then, mark the
example as illustrative and describe the screenshot requirements without
implying the captures or completed production outcomes exist.

The opening should state the outcome and show a short version of the process:
select a customer problem, gather product proof, write one message brief,
adapt it into distinct assets, review, publish, and evaluate.

## Questions and article outline

1. **Which customer question is worth answering?** Gather real questions from
   support, conversations, and available search evidence. Select one practical
   takeaway. Use a product capability as proof only when it helps explain the
   answer, and provide a checklist for choosing a useful topic.
2. **What belongs in the message brief?** Give a copyable template with
   audience, problem, capability, proof, limitation, and next action. Fill
   every field for the example.
3. **How does the message change across formats?** Show a short opening plus
   product-demo outline for Hook video, a concise customer-question answer for
   Wall of text, and a five-slide explanation for Slideshows. Each asset should
   do a different job while keeping product claims consistent.
4. **What does a realistic week look like?** Separate preparation, review,
   publishing, and response review. Three useful posts can occupy a week;
   avoid prescribing daily posting as a requirement.
5. **What must be checked before publishing?** Verify factual claims,
   screenshot accuracy, media rights, captions, destination links, and the
   actual supported product workflow.
6. **How do we know whether it helped?** Explain how reader questions, useful
   responses, and task completion can reveal clarity or gaps. Where available,
   assess relevant search visits and engagement. Track product clicks, signups,
   and activation when the post's purpose supports that next step. Give a blank
   tracking template rather than fabricated results.

Conclude with an appropriate next action after the reader has received the
templates and example. A product link is optional and should help apply the
method. Retain useful FAQs that answer remaining questions.

## Example planning template

| Slot | Work | Deliverable |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Monday | Choose one customer question and gather evidence | Filled message brief and useful source example |
| Tuesday | Draft and review a step-by-step explanation | Five-slide outline or equivalent instructional asset |
| Wednesday | Publish the explanation and collect questions | Published asset and question log |
| Thursday | Demonstrate the same capability visually | Short walkthrough script with verified screen references |
| Friday | Publish the walkthrough | Published video and useful response notes |
| Weekend or next workday | Answer one recurring question and review outcomes | FAQ post and updated tracking sheet |

This is an illustrative cadence, not a promised time saving or a required
posting frequency. Choose formats the founder can actually produce.

## Screenshot requirements

The user will supply actual product captures. The draft should identify where
each capture helps explain a decision:

- The real feature brief and owned product demo used for the example.
- Hook's saved opening and Demo step showing the two segments.
- Wall's manual text editor and the resulting text-led video.
- Slideshows' ordered sequence and correction of one individual slide.
- The saved outputs and relevant scheduling screen for a verified platform.

Use descriptive captions and alt text. Exclude private account details. Do not
claim unsupported formats, platforms, automation, time savings, or outcomes.
Slideshows saves an image sequence; do not call it a slideshow-video exporter.
Manual video text is not automatic speech transcription. Product captures in
the article do not establish an automatic screenshot-import feature.

## Publication and evaluation

Before publication, record the guide's available Search Console indexing,
query, impression, click, and average-position data. Record organic landing
visits, guide-to-product clicks, completed signups, and activation when
analytics is connected. Label unavailable data rather than filling gaps.

After publication, run the existing public SEO checks and inspect the deployed
URL and Google-selected canonical in Search Console. Compare subsequent
performance with the baseline using the same filters and periods. Review
direction after roughly four weeks and extend observation when samples are
small. Record confounders such as other releases or promotions; do not claim
that an article update caused a ranking change merely because the two occur
together.

Success means readers can reproduce the example and relevant search visitors
progress toward product evaluation. Impressions and page counts alone do not
establish business value.
