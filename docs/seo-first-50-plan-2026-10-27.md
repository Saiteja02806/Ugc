## Why the first two lead

The existing product-demo guide covers selection, editing and review after footage exists. A founder still needs to decide exactly what to say and capture readable evidence. The script guide supplies an original copyable two-column worksheet plus one clearly fictional software example; the recording guide supplies a capture decision tree, native recording sources, a rehearsal sequence and pass/fail checklist. They cross-link and link back to the editing guide. No customer footage, provider generation or new UI dependencies are required.

Alternative considered: a storyboard page. Defer because it could repeat the script worksheet without original annotated frames. Another broad “AI UGC for SaaS” page would compete with the commercial generator and existing explainer; do not add one. Competitor-comparison work remains paused.
