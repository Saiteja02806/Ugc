## 1. What UGC Pilot does

Working product description:

> UGC Pilot helps founders turn their business context and product media into short social content, adapt suitable formats, review and edit the results, and schedule supported posts.

The product has several connected parts. Articles should explain the part that answers the reader's question, while keeping the overall journey understandable.

| Part | User task | Evidence and editorial boundary |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Business context | Give the product a website, offer and audience to work from | The public homepage describes this starting point. Context can guide copy; it does not prove every output is accurate. |
| Trending / daily content | Review recurring business-aware content and select useful work | Current homepage explicitly features daily slideshows. Pricing describes a wider daily feed. Do not equate concepts, reviewed posts and custom model generations. Confirm the actual deliverable for each format before quantifying it. |
| Explore | Start with a reference or chosen format | `lib/explore/workflows.ts` exposes Talking Head + Demo, Hook video, Wall of text and Slideshows. A visible source route is not proof that every current production step has passed acceptance. |
| Text remix and creative skills | Adapt hook words, longer overlays, slideshow copy or footage directions to the business | Text, a video prompt and a finished video are different outputs. Drafting a prompt alone does not generate media. |
| AI Studio / characters | Generate custom images and video with supported models and references | Generation uses separate credits. Do not promise identical identity, exact reference recreation, unlimited outputs or successful product-interface generation. |
| Editing / finishing / library | Reuse owned media, add exact text, trim, compose an opening with a real product demo, save and recover work | Use the final exported file as evidence. Existing source records contain recent quality/release gaps; do not advertise a local fix as deployed. |
| Scheduling | Choose accounts, content, destination and timing | Describe supported format/account combinations explicitly. Reviewing a post personally is different from a team approval system. |
| AI-client integration | Use supported UGC Pilot operations from a compatible connected client | The 17 October MCP audit documents partial website parity. It does not support advertising end-to-end social publishing from the plugin. Recheck newer server capabilities before writing integration tutorials. |

### Messaging questions to resolve before publication

1. Homepage names Instagram, TikTok and YouTube; pricing mainly names Instagram and YouTube. Confirm current supported post types and account requirements, then make the copy consistent. Do not infer that TikTok is unavailable merely because pricing omits it.
2. Distinguish daily concepts, ready content, references and custom generation credits. Monthly price divided by daily concepts is not the cost of an AI-generated finished video.
3. Explain automatic preparation versus a user's final scheduling decision. Do not promise unattended posting unless that exact mode exists and is verified.
4. The homepage's 60-second readiness and performance-oriented wording need a reproducible basis or more precise wording. No end-to-end timing or engagement benchmark was established here.
5. Reference recreation currently has recorded identity/scene and slideshow-fidelity limitations. Use actual examples, disclose remaining manual correction, and avoid exact-copy promises.

This supports investigating comparative intent, not copying their tables or treating them as reliable feature evidence. Use the official source for each factual cell; mark absent information **not verified**, rather than **not supported**.

## 4. Campaign: help a founder choose and complete an app-content workflow

Working campaign theme: **From an app demo to content worth publishing.**

Positioning hypothesis: a focused path from the founder's real product context to several reviewable social formats. Test this with users and output examples; it is not a claim that competitors lack business context or review controls.

Content must cover discovery, choice, completion and evaluation. A comparison page alone cannot establish trust, and educational traffic alone does not establish purchase intent.

### Comparison method

Use the same permitted assets and founder brief across tools: the same product URL/context, screenshots, a short real screen recording and the same factual product claim. Choose comparable deliverables, not different outputs under a shared label.

Record:

1. What inputs each tool actually accepts and what it produces.
2. Product/message fidelity, exact text, editable elements, demo handling and format choice.
3. Active human work, elapsed processing time, attempts/failures and time spent fixing output.
4. Actual provider/credit expenditure separately from subscription price. Count usable finished outputs with a stated rubric.
5. Export quality and supported publishing format/account combinations, separating a direct publish from export/manual handoff.
6. Who each tool suits, when UGC Pilot is a worse fit, current plan restrictions and the test date.

Disclosure: UGC Pilot publishes its own comparison. Link competitor facts to official sources, include original captures for claims based on testing, and clearly label untested/documented features. Do not claim we tested tools based on reading their websites. Spending or third-party trials would be separate execution work.

[Google's review guidance](https://developers.google.com/search/docs/specialty/ecommerce/write-high-quality-reviews) supports firsthand evidence, relevant measurements and balanced decision factors. This is a quality standard, not a ranking promise.
