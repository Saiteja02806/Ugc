## Why the new brief pushed in the wrong direction

- It said to rebuild the scenario and prioritize a concrete business detail. In these outputs, that became describing what the product does instead of adapting the source's social or emotional moment.
- It discouraged EVERYTHING and unexplained this as generic. In a short reaction hook, exaggeration and a temporarily withheld object can be deliberate devices, especially when a following demo supplies the answer. They should not be removed categorically.
- It over-weighted brevity and literal feature clarity. V4 addressed a length rejection while making several outputs less expressive. Passing schema/length checks says nothing about preserving the hook's appeal.
- It added multiple instructions about AI partners and approval. The final outputs became more uniform. This is an observed association consistent with the wording of the brief, not an isolated causal experiment on each instruction.
- The original concern about false claims was valid, but the correction was too broad creatively. A fictional POV setup or metaphor is not automatically a factual testimonial. Protect against unsupported lived experience while retaining clearly playful scenarios. Likewise, 😭 can express colloquial overwhelmed excitement; replacing it based only on a literal sad/happy category can lose the voice.

## Corrected direction

Start by describing what must survive from the source: the situation, implied relationship, emotional reversal, curiosity gap, punchline position, rhythm, casing, punctuation and useful line breaks. Preserve those elements unless the user requests a different style. Adapt the subject only far enough to make that same moment plausible for the business. A product capability can supply the payoff without being recited in the hook. AI, SaaS and the brand name are optional words, not mandatory ingredients.

Reject a candidate when it could replace several different source hooks unchanged, when it reads like a feature bullet or when its POV/question prefix is the only remaining connection to the source. Check these creative criteria before rewarding specificity or shorter length. Keep truthful claims, valid timing and readable bounds as requirements, but do not mistake them for writing quality.

This is a revised brief direction, not an implemented v5 prompt or another generated comparison. A small set of human-reviewed, source-faithful examples should establish the intended taste before another model experiment. No additional paid round is queued.
