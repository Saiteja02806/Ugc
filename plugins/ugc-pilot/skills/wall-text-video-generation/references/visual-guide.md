The text carries the message; the background need not mime every sentence. A soft-white, yellow or brand-selected overlay can sit upper, central or lower depending on reference and contrast. Prefer calm contrast behind dense type rather than an unnecessarily empty text box. Keep a visible block inside safe margins for the target layout and avoid platform controls; exact geometry must be previewed by the editor.

Timeline beats cover the full duration with no gaps/overlaps. For a continuous seven-second shot, one 0–7-second sustained-action beat is valid. Montage cuts do not reset or rewrite the overlay. A long paragraph in six seconds is usually a replay/pause style, not a claim of full first-pass readability. Offer a longer edit if wanted, preserving every word.
