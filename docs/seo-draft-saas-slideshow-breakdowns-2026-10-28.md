**Unpublished planning draft.** Original evidence required before release: Owned full carousels with exact copy, visual explanations and limitations. Fictional examples are labeled; they are not actual customer outcomes.

## Select an owned complete carousel

An example breakdown requires the full slide sequence and permission to reproduce or describe it. Record the original export, source of each asset, approved exact copy and the reason it can be shared. A cropped opening alone cannot show whether the rest of the story satisfies its promise.

## Annotate each slide’s job

For each slide identify its message, visual, place in the sequence and relationship to the product fact. Note whether the final slide gives a relevant next step. If an actual slide is unclear or makes an unsupported promise, show the approved correction and its real review notes.

## Fictional planning sequence

A sample concept for a fictional notes app could move from a post-call situation to opening a project note, finding a next-step section and reviewing the action. This is an illustrative structure only; it is not an owned carousel, a screenshot of software or a published campaign.

## Publish only verified examples

Provide actual permissioned slides, exact text and limitations. Check phone legibility and whether interface labels reflect the current product. Do not invent customer quotes, performance figures or a completed before-and-after review.
