# Performance guide

Adapted from the supplied Hook Video Generation document. No clip audit files or measured retention data were supplied with that document; these are directing recipes, not independently verified observations.

| Map | 0–0.7 seconds | 0.7–1.4 seconds | 1.4–2.4 seconds | 2.4–3 seconds |
| --- | --- | --- | --- | --- |
| Immediate reaction | Start with a legible reaction | Eyes/brows make a small change | One motivated gesture | Hold a readable expression |
| Discovery | Focus on a phone or off-screen target | Gaze pauses, brows register a change | Emotion emerges with one gesture | Sustain the result |
| Skeptical | Mild confusion during an ordinary task | Squint or one brow lifts | Small head tilt or palm-up question | Knowing look toward camera |
| Relief | Held anticipation or tension | Eyes and mouth soften | Relaxed exhale or discreet nod | Natural small smile |

Match the hero action to the starting pose: glasses can lower only when worn; a phone remains held consistently; an uncluttered hand can lift toward the mouth. Do not jump, spin, cry and laugh in the same three-second shot. Surprising upside can move from disbelief to interested delight; repetitive work can show fatigue; a counterintuitive feature can produce skepticism to curiosity. Do not make every product cause extreme shock.

Intensity 1 uses a micro-smile, eye change or light head tilt. Intensity 2 makes the same progression clearer with one short gesture. Intensity 3 permits a larger reaction only when context supports it. Preserve the same recognizable face, hair, skin tone and wardrobe when using a reference; that instruction cannot guarantee the generator's output.

Illustrative three-second prompt: “Vertical 9:16 realistic smartphone footage, three seconds. An adult creator sits beside a phone in natural window light. Medium close-up, steady framing. 0–0.7s: focused gaze at the phone; 0.7–1.4s: one brow lifts and the gaze pauses; 1.4–2.4s: a small head tilt and restrained smile; 2.4–3s: hold the curious smile. Keep anatomy, clothing, phone and lighting consistent. No baked-in words, dialogue, music, effects or voiceover.” This is a draft, not a generated video.
