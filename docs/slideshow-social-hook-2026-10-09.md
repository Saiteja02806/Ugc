# Social slideshow hook refinement — 9 October 2026

Implemented locally in both existing Carousel structures. Deployment and
authenticated production acceptance are pending.

## Result

Slide 1 is one statement about one main idea, in one text block. Writers aim
for 6–14 words and two to four display lines. The hard accepted word range is
5–14, retaining compact hooks. A specific problem, changed belief, tension,
or honest promise leaves the explanation for Slide 2 onward. Short first-person
hooks are explicitly allowed. A list promise must match the value the following
slides actually deliver.

The cover uses fixed 84px Inter Tight Bold 700, up from 72px. Content headings
remain 50px and body copy 48px SemiBold. The measured four-line cover maximum
remains authoritative; overflowing copy is rewritten, never shrunk or
truncated. Covers have no subtitle, explanatory body, heading pill, or CTA.
The shared constant also updates the editor preview.

Both generation validators reject separate sentences or paragraphs as
`hook_structure`. This catches a compact failure such as `content felt hard.
i needed a change`, even though it fits the word budget. Single semantic line
breaks, common abbreviations, domains, and decimal numbers remain valid.
Curiosity and semantic one-idea quality are writing guidance rather than a
mechanical engagement test. No hardcoded replacement hook is introduced.

The same guidance reaches initial, batch, full-plan, targeted, and native-cover
repair calls. Structure 2 repairs only a rejected cover when its body slides
are valid. Existing format IDs, six-slide roles, body budgets, optional content
headings, image selection, and saved render bytes retain their existing
contracts. Intentional new renders use the new renderer versions.

Versions: Structure 1 writer v50, Structure 2 writer v21;
`social-single-statement-hook-inter-tight-v26` and
`story-native-single-statement-hook-inter-tight-v12` renderers.

  generation structures.

  within two to four lines in both structures and both 1:1 and 4:5 sizes.
