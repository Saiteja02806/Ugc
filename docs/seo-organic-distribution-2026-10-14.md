## Product-demo tutorial

A product demo does not need to explain every feature at once.

Choose one question your audience asks. Record the smallest real product action that answers it. Give the opening a clear job, then leave enough time for the viewer to understand the result.

Example: for an app that groups lesson notes by student, show that group. You do not also need billing, setup and every menu in the same clip.

Before sharing, watch the export on a phone with and without sound. Check the crop, the actual product evidence and the exact overlay words.

Tutorial: https://getugcpilot.com/guides/product-demo-to-short-video

## Readable-video-text tutorial

Text that looks clear on a desktop can become hard to read in a phone feed.

Try three checks on your next export:

- Can you read the message at normal speed without pausing?
- Does it remain clear when the background moves?
- Can you still see the useful product action and the platform controls?

If approved wording must stay exact, give it more time or a calmer layout. Do not silently remove words. Review speech captions separately from the headline.

Checklist and illustrative comparison: https://getugcpilot.com/guides/readable-video-text

## Weekly-content guide

No new feature to announce this week? Start with a real customer question.

Write the answer before choosing a format. A demonstration can show an action, a paragraph can explain a decision and a slide sequence can separate the steps. Choose only the formats that help the reader.
