## Understand the reference before adapting

Record the source and the role of each segment: opening, context, proof and ending. Separate a creative structure from the reference creator’s words, footage, music, identity and product claims. A public reference does not automatically provide permission to reuse any of its assets.

## Build from your own verified facts

Write the intended viewer task, your actual feature and the product action a recording can show. For a fictional notes app, the supported planning fact could be that a call note contains a next-step section. A reference about automatically sending follow-ups cannot be copied into this product concept unless that feature genuinely exists.

## Write an original adaptation

A hypothetical new sequence might be “wait, the next step is right here,” followed by an independently recorded note and a readable section. Write original words, use your own footage or properly licensed assets, and verify any conditions. This is a concept plan, not an analysis of a specific permissioned reference or a finished asset.

## Document the real transformation

Before treating the article as an example, provide a permissioned source, a source analysis, a verified product brief and an approved original final asset. Check exact copy, private data and rights. Do not promise that your adaptation will reproduce another creator’s results.
