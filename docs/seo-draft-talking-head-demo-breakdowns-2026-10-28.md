# Draft: Talking-head-plus-demo examples: what each scene contributes

**Unpublished planning draft.** Original evidence required before release: Permissioned full sequences, transcripts, app evidence and annotations. Fictional examples are labeled; they are not actual customer outcomes.

## Select a complete permitted sequence

A breakdown needs an actual complete talking-head and product demonstration, not an isolated transcript. Record the creator, source, rights, export version and relevant product feature. Remove confidential data before sharing frames or clips.

## Map speech to the screen

For each segment, record the actual spoken words, time range, visible screen and resulting action. Highlight where the screen demonstrates the claim and where a statement lacks visible support. A time-coded table should use measured timestamps from the actual asset.

## Fictional planning example

An illustrative script could say “open the call note and check the next-step section” while the screen shows that action. This is a hypothetical example, not a real transcript or recorded video. A generated presenter must not be represented as a customer giving a personal endorsement.

## Document the real review

Save the permissioned source, transcript, screenshots and any correction with approval dates. Check captions, audio and screen readability on a phone. Do not infer retention, customer results or tested improvements without actual data.
