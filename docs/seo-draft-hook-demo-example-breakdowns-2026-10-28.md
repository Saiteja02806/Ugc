# Draft: Hook-plus-demo examples for software: annotated complete sequences

**Unpublished planning draft.** Original evidence required before release: Permissioned full videos with shot-specific explanations, not a hook list. Fictional examples are labeled; they are not actual customer outcomes.

## What makes a useful breakdown

A useful breakdown identifies the exact hook, the first product frame and the visible action that answers the opening. Use a complete video you own or have permission to analyze and share. A list of hooks cannot show whether the later product view actually proves the opening.

## Illustrative shot plan

For a fictional notes app, a planning sequence might be: reaction with “the next steps are right here”; a project screen; a call note opening; and a held view of a next-step section. This is a written example, not a real clip, a customer story or an observed result. Confirm that your real app supports the same behavior before using it.

## Annotate an actual asset

For each shot record its start and end time, the exact text, the screen action and why it follows the previous shot. Capture a readable frame only when you have rights to reproduce it. Mark any unsupported claim or missing context and describe the actual revision, rather than presenting a hypothetical correction as a completed edit.

## Review before publication

Check the final video for readable product proof, permissioned footage, accurate text and removed private data. Save the original asset, annotated frames and review notes. Do not claim that an example produced views or conversions without verified evidence.
