# Draft: Turn business context into a native-looking product slideshow

**Unpublished planning draft.** Original evidence required before release: Actual brief-to-copy-to-reviewed-slides example. Fictional examples are labeled; they are not actual customer outcomes.

## Turn a brief into one reader task

Collect the audience, the situation, the product behavior and the intended next action. Check each product fact against the current interface. A business context profile helps organize the brief, but its contents should be verified before appearing in marketing copy.

## Fictional brief and slide copy

For a fictional notes app: audience, a founder after a call; feature, a project note with a next-step section; task, finding the recorded next action. Five possible slides: “the call is over”; “where is the next step?”; “open the project note”; “check the next-step section”; “start with the action you recorded.” This is a planning example, not an actual reviewed carousel.

## Create the visual instructions

Use a suitable owned or licensed visual for the situation and a real sanitized product screenshot for the feature. Specify which screen and crop demonstrate the action. Do not represent a generated interface as the real product or imply automatic sending when the notes app only displays text.

## Verify the actual output

Before release compare each exported slide against the approved brief and exact words. Record the source and permission for every visual, check legibility on a phone and test the ending destination. Mark the guide’s real brief-to-reviewed-slides example incomplete until those artifacts exist.
